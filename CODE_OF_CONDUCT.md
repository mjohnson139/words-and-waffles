# Code of conduct

Words & Waffles is a small, friendly project. We want it to stay that way.

- Be kind. Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or put-downs of people, whatever their skill, background or experience.
- Keep discussion about the work: the game, the code, the docs and the ideas.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Explain your reasons.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who do not follow this code. We try to act in the open and with care.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the GitHub repository, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
