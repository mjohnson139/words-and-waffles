## Description

Good morning! Words & Waffles is a cosy daily word game with a warm breakfast twist. Every day there is one five-letter word to find, and you have six tries to guess it. After each guess the tiles tell you what you have got: a tile in the right pocket is in the right spot, a tile in the wrong pocket is in the recipe but in the wrong place, and a dark tile is not in the recipe at all. Keep your daily streak going and your stack of waffles grows taller. Want more to chew on? Seconds serve up extra words whenever you want another helping. Pick a word lets you type a secret word and hand the phone to a friend to guess. Look back on your games in History, check your numbers on Stats, and earn little badges called toppings as you play. Words & Waffles works offline, so you can play on the bus or in a field. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The whole app is open source under the MIT License and lives on GitHub, so you can read every line of it yourself. Pull up a chair and enjoy your breakfast of words.
